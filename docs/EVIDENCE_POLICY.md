# Evidence and content policy

1. Preserve the original publication/source URL and bibliographic metadata.
2. Distinguish study findings from editorial interpretation.
3. Identify study design, population, duration and limitations.
4. Do not treat observational association as proof of causation.
5. Do not convert relative risk changes into years of life gained.
6. Avoid unsupported supplement, drug or anti-ageing claims.
7. Provide a last-reviewed date and correction history in the full release.
8. Treat starter summaries as educational drafts requiring editorial review.
9. Never present the application as a diagnostic or treatment tool.
