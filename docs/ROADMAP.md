# Roadmap

- LS1 Foundation: dashboard, local database, research library, daily habits.
- LS2 Research Intelligence: taxonomy, study metadata, evidence assessments.
- LS3 Personal Workspace: profile, goals, journal and preferences.
- LS4 Healthy Ageing: nutrition, movement, sleep and brain-health content.
- LS5 Analytics: trends, reports and exports.
- LS6 AI Intelligence: local RAG assistant with citations.
- LS7 Product Polish: accessibility, localisation, offline support and security review.
- LS8 Desktop Edition: Electron packaging and installers.
